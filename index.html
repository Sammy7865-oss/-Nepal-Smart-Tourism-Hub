Culture

The Nepal between the mountains

Over 125 ethnic groups, 120+ languages, and a festival calendar that never really stops.

Dashain

Nepal's longest festival — fifteen days of family reunions, kite flying, and blessings from elders.

Tihar

The festival of lights, honouring crows, dogs, cows, and siblings across five distinct days.

Holi

The color festival — Kathmandu's streets erupt in powder pigment to welcome spring.

Momo

Steamed or fried dumplings filled with buffalo, chicken, or vegetables — Nepal's favourite street food.

Dal Bhat

Lentil soup, rice, and vegetable curry — the daily meal that powers every trek in the country.

Newari Khaja Set

A Kathmandu Valley specialty thali of beaten rice, spiced potatoes, and buffalo delicacies.

Prayer Flags

Five-colour flags carry mantras on the wind across every high pass and monastery roof.

Newari Craftsmanship

Wood-carved windows and pagoda roofs, a building tradition unbroken for over a thousand years.

Sherpa Hospitality

High-altitude tea houses run by families who have hosted trekkers for generations.
