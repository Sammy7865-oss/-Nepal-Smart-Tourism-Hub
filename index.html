Smart tools

Plan around altitude, weather, and season

The tools this hub is built around — not just a list of places, but the logistics that actually change a Nepal trip.

Itinerary builder

Drag regions onto a timeline and the route reorders itself by elevation, so you never climb 4,000m in a day by accident.

Live trail conditions

Weather and pass status for trekking routes above 3,000m, pulled in daily during high season.

Cultural calendar

Festival dates like Dashain, Tihar, and Holi mapped against each region so travel plans don't collide with closures.

Traveler reports

Short, dated notes from other visitors on trail difficulty, road conditions, and altitude sickness at each stop.

Beyond the mountains

What's happening on the ground

Nepal's culture shifts as fast as its elevation does — four things worth timing a trip around.

Sept–Oct

Dashain

The longest and most significant festival, marked by family gatherings and kite-filled skies over the valley.

Everyday

Newari cuisine

Yomari, choila, and bara — Kathmandu Valley food traditions with their own festival calendar.

March

Holi

Color-throwing celebrations that start a week early in the hills and peak on the same day nationwide.

Year-round

Living heritage

Pottery Square, woodcarving workshops, and thangka painting studios still active in daily Kathmandu life.
